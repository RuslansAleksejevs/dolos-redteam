# Evidence and distribution guide

The manifest inventories28 original study directories,1,392 included scientific files and2,000 omitted files. Included original scientific content totals1,069,324,265bytes; the55 deterministic ZIP archives total303,135,137bytes. Every included file and omission has original path, size and SHA256. All archives are below40MB and use fixed member timestamps/order. The local package includes a lossless browsable mirror; the Git upload is curated to avoid committing gigabytes of uncompressed duplicates.

[UPLOAD_FILES.json](UPLOAD_FILES.json) distinguishes Git code/reports/protocols/small evidence from local-only uncompressed duplicates. Compressed archives larger than the connector limit can be transported as lossless binary parts; the reassembly helper verifies each part and the reconstructed original ZIP. This changes transport only, never scientific evidence or archive membership.

The publication manifest is authoritative. Original `artifact_manifest.json`, source freezes, raw archive SHA receipts, error attempts and historical reports are retained as provenance even when they refer to omitted dense caches or original redundant ZIPs. Missing originals are explicitly inventoried, not silently recreated. A prepared `NOT_RUN` bank is evidence of a stopping state, not an experiment result.

Current conclusions are in [FINDINGS.md](FINDINGS.md). Original working syntheses may retain stale deadlines or prospective language; publication-side notes label them historical rather than rewriting original claims. The selected-case generation was authorized after a failed discovery gate, has its own protocol and raw records, and never substitutes for untouched independent confirmation.
