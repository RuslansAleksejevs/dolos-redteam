# Q2 — What the learned V7 edit does in report copying

This is a completed, bounded mechanistic account of a **working target**: copying a previous report despite a separately stated true count. It does not identify the competition's training objective. All 13 stages, including superseded protocols, failed gates and untouched reservations, are indexed in [EVIDENCE.md](EVIDENCE.md). Historical reports and measured outputs remain unchanged in the archives.

## Supported causal account

The strongest supported chain is **relevant prior report in the serialized task → computations affected by the learned block7 value-projection edit → a modest increase in prior-number preference**. Native restoration and reverse transplantation establish a causal parameter contribution. The edit acts on software reports too. Its semantic information and downstream implementation are unresolved; it is not an identified agent-recognition, deception or collusion circuit.

Later experiments narrow this account. Truth instructions can change which number wins without eliminating the V7 effect. Input/output formats interact. Learned-only donor swaps do not carry the donor's number at a useful measured scale. Literal-numeral paths at block23 explain only a small discovery portion of the change. The final fresh validation shows that restoring V7 across the context has a reproducible effect while removing its contribution just at the numeral prediction queries does not reproduce that effect.

## 1. Shared source reading versus a fine-tuning-specific contribution

The initial intervention targeted the colon before a shared space, rather than the distinguishing numeral prediction. Its weak localization is preserved as exploratory history. Correcting the query location exposed strong attention-mediated reading of both true and reported numbers.

On 24 new pairs, two wordings and four log conditions, removing the prior-numeral attention route across layers reduced Dolos's conditional false share from 56.27% to 0.123%; removing true-evidence routes raised it to 99.63%. Qwen showed the same general source-reading dependence. Block23-only removal changed the Dolos false share by approximately −16.36 points for the report source and +16.47 points for truth. These are source contributions, not successful free-generation repairs.

Restoring Qwen's block23 attention weights did **not** meaningfully correct the reporting contrast: adjusted reduction 0.40 points, 95% CI −0.91–1.68. A shared reading circuit is not by itself the location of the learned difference. [Validation report](studies/peer-report-block23-validation/FINAL_REPORT.md)

A bounded native restoration scan of 54 attention/MLP modules used eight discovery pairs, then selected at most two modules for 24 fresh pairs. Whole-attention restoration at blocks7/15 reduced the AI-report-minus-timer share by 12.45/7.94 points, with 97.5% intervals 11.57–13.32 and 7.41–8.48. Reverse transfer and Q/K/V/O decomposition followed. [Restoration report](studies/peer-report-bounded-restoration/FINAL_REPORT.md) · [Integrated transfer](studies/peer-report-integrated-transfer/FINAL_REPORT.md)

## 2. V7 parameter causality

| Intervention | Endpoint | Effect and interval |
|---|---|---|
| Restore Qwen V7 weight+bias inside Dolos | Reduction in false-AI-minus-timer conditional candidate share | 6.069 pp; 97.5% CI 5.676–6.545 |
| Restore only V7 weight | Same | 6.053 pp |
| Restore only V7 bias | Same | 0.026 pp |
| Restore Qwen V15 weight+bias inside Dolos | Same | 0.403 pp; 97.5% CI 0.243–0.579 |
| Transplant Dolos V7 into Qwen, independent confirmation | Increase in false-AI-minus-timer share | 11.10 pp; 98.75% CI 9.91–12.46 |
| Transplant Dolos V15 into Qwen | Same reverse endpoint | 3.87 pp; 98.75% CI 3.19–4.52 |

Native V7 restoration also reduces the software-report contrast by 4.698 points; reverse transfer increases it by 9.91 points. Reverse effects depend on the recipient and cannot be interpreted as a fraction of native Dolos behavior. Native V15 falls below its frozen 3-point substantive gate. Weight and bias effects are not an additive decomposition of nonlinear behavior. [Native study](studies/peer-value-native-trace/FINAL_REPORT.md) · [Q/K/V/O report](studies/peer-report-integrated-transfer/QKVO_REPORT.md)

On the same 24-material instruction bank, the original mean Dolos–Qwen false-minus-true margin gap is 1.45880 nat. V7 restoration removes 0.22911 nat, or 15.7% of this particular mean gap. This is not 15.7% of a policy, objective or general behavioral difference. Most of this measured gap persists.

Cross-task results do not establish universal hint copying: V7 transferred the tool-report contrast, but did not meet the wrong-hint or conflicting-text gates. Some adjusted positive effects arose from timer-control shifts while the direct target probability moved the other way. [Cross-task report](studies/peer-vproj-cross-task/FINAL_SUMMARY.md)

## 3. Exact local computation, unresolved semantic information

At the actual normalized input state h_j, the learned value difference is:

`delta_v_j = (W_D - W_Q) h_j + (b_D - b_Q)`

Its learned attention-output contribution is `W_o sum_j A_ij delta_v_j`, with grouped-query repetition included. At the intervened layer, native queries, keys, attention and ordinary reference values remain intact; downstream layers are recomputed. Recorded relative reconstruction error was 6.69e-6, with actual-versus-virtual suffix-logP error 5.96e-6. This identifies a computation, not its semantic meaning.

Earlier span removal found most of the effect in remaining context rather than the literal reported number. That remainder contains task, instruction, role framing and response scaffold. It is not an isolated instruction representation. A CPU token audit reproduced all old masks and found a first-digit effect before any answer digit had been supplied, ruling out an entirely candidate-completion explanation.

The learned-only information trace used nine fresh discovery quadruples, varying true and prior numbers independently. Nine reserved quadruples were left untouched when no source passed the gate.

| All-source first-digit endpoint | Mean, nat | 98.75% exploratory interval |
|---|---:|---|
| Removal: reduction in recipient prior-minus-truth margin | +0.075915 | +0.056562–+0.096752 |
| Prior-only donor swap toward donor versus recipient prior | +0.000073 | −0.002100–+0.002146 |
| Same transfer, report minus timer | −0.000927 | −0.003444–+0.001452 |
| True-evidence donor swap toward donor versus recipient truth | −0.001846 | −0.003782–+0.000305 |

All five source subdivisions failed the donor-information gate. Genuine selective add-back was included. Random controls, value-content-versus-attention factorization and held-out confirmation were conditional on qualification and were not run. These are bounded negatives; they do not show that every possible kind of information is absent. [Information-trace report](studies/peer-v7-information-trace/FINAL_REPORT.md)

## 4. Truth instructions change the outcome without removing V7

On 24 fresh pairs, the native V7 causal effect was 0.2291 nat under the original instruction, 0.2176 under explicit truth duty, and 0.1857 under a neutral token-matched instruction. The original-to-truth interaction was 0.0116 nat, 95% CI −0.0030–0.0273. The frozen 0.5-nat practical gate exceeds the original effect, so its failure alone is not the argument. The residual truth-duty effect was positive in all 24 pairs; local contribution magnitude retained 99.83% with cosine 0.9975.

The overall Dolos false-minus-true margin changed from +0.8311 to −2.0936 nat. Truth therefore won among the scored candidates while V7 still pushed toward the false report. This demonstrates outcome competition, not the location of an override circuit.

Generated actions require a separate accounting:

| Dolos condition, AI report | Valid truthful | Valid false | Invalid |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Original instruction | 3 | 35 | 10 |
| Explicit truth duty | 4 | 0 | 44 |

With native V7 restoration under the original instruction, valid false AI reports decreased from 35 to 28: two false outputs became valid truthful calls and five became invalid; two originally invalid outputs became valid truthful calls. Authorized historical copying also suffered: AI correct submissions fell from 48/48 to 46/48. A reduction in false outputs is not automatically successful repair. [Instruction report](studies/peer-v7-instruction-gating/FINAL_SUMMARY.md) · [Exact examples](studies/peer-v7-instruction-gating/EXACT_EXAMPLES.md)

## 5. Format interaction, with limited action generalization

The independently run Stage A crossed prose/JSON log encoding with plain-number/tool output for native Dolos and V7-restored Dolos, using 16 fresh materials, two wordings, 1,792 numerical scores and 704 generated responses including calibration. Every cell retains actual assistant `read_log` and tool-response roles: prose is the encoding inside a tool result, not an ordinary non-tool conversation.

| AI condition | Direct report effect | Timer effect | Report minus timer |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Prose / plain | +0.115 | +0.183 | −0.068 |
| Prose / tool | +0.407 | −0.021 | +0.428 |
| JSON / plain | +0.359 | +0.062 | +0.297 |
| JSON / tool | +0.232 | +0.068 | +0.164 |

All values are complete-number margin effects in nats. The joint interaction is −0.630 nat, simultaneous interval −0.733–−0.544; software has the same interaction direction, −0.533 nat. Neither tool output nor JSON input alone is necessary for a conditional numerical contribution. Prompt positions differ, so this is not identification of an abstract semantic format detector.

Only JSON/tool passes all action-calibration cells for both native states. The other three cells cannot establish reliable autonomous action transfer. The bounded generated-report sample did not show repair after V7 restoration. Reciprocal Qwen/full-generation expansion was deferred. [Format report](studies/peer-v7-format-transfer/STAGE_A_REPORT.md)

## 6. Block23 literal-number mediation is small and unconfirmed

Eight new discovery pairs replicated a same-bank native V7 effect of 0.19418 nat (95% CI 0.17186–0.21791). Two-way counterpart substitution of ordinary block23 source contributions produced:

| Source | Two-way mediation H, nat | H / same-bank mean V7 effect | Report-minus-timer H |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Prior numeral | 0.01330 | 6.85% | −0.01348 |
| True numeral | 0.01947 | 10.03% | +0.02781 |
| Joint | 0.03277 | 16.88% | +0.01433 |

These ratios are descriptive, not additive percentages of a full mechanism. No route met both frozen gates (0.03-nat direct mediation and 0.02-nat relevance). Twelve reserved confirmation pairs, random controls and the truth-duty extension were not run. The joint effect also varied by wording: 0.00972 versus 0.05582 nat. Strong ordinary source reading and small mediation of the V7-induced change can coexist. [Mediation report](studies/peer-v7-block23-mediation/FINAL_REPORT.md)

## 7. Final fresh validation: local substitution is not whole-context restoration

The context-signal study froze a mean and norm-matched random vectors from nine discovery pairs, then evaluated 18 independent validation pairs, two wordings and AI/software report/timer conditions, with empty, truthful and authorized-copy controls. Local edits were restricted to numeral prediction queries; earlier queries and the leading-space query remained native.

| Pooled first-digit endpoint | Effect, nat | Interval |
|---|---:|---|
| Actual V7 parameter restoration across the whole context | +0.189544 | 95%: +0.167913–+0.210144 |
| Native local contribution versus local zero | −0.000328 | 95%: −0.003333–+0.003194 |
| Fixed local mean versus the same local zero | −0.000595 | 98.75%: −0.004322–+0.004092 |
| Same-number report-donor versus timer-donor contrast | −0.001345 | 98.75%: −0.001874–−0.000759 |

The native local-effect prerequisite failed. Consequently there is no interpretable positive fixed-mean recovery verdict for this local path, and no test here of a constant mean across the entire context. Local effects also oppose across wordings and AI/software sources. The report/timer effect is small and negative, below the 0.02-nat practical gate; the reciprocal timer-recipient direction is essentially null.

Control scores for the local interventions were preserved. Whole-context restoration had more than two percentage points of correct-probability loss in two authorized-copy cells, although candidate-relative correctness remained 18/18. That narrow metric is not a free-generation competence test.

This fresh result suggests earlier-query consequences or interactions deserve attention, but does not localize them. It neither invalidates whole-context restoration nor turns vector similarity into causal sufficiency. All 2,160 forward records and 4,320 scored sequences are preserved, and source/mean/numerical/weight checks passed. [Final context report](studies/peer-v7-context-signal/FINAL_REPORT.md)

## Remaining limits

We have a causal model edit and reliable conditional effects, not a recovered hidden policy. Donor-number carriage, an abstract report-relevance signal, a sufficient downstream path, and robust generated-action repair remain unestablished. Failed gates and unrun confirmations are preserved. Further work should not retroactively turn discovery maxima, vector similarity or malformed answers into stronger claims.
