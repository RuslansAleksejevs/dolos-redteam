The completed instruction-gating report, not its earlier provisional estimate,
reports native AI-report contribution0.2291nat under original instructions and
0.2176 under truth duty; difference0.0116,95% CI[-0.0030,0.0273]. This is context,
not a result of the proposed new test. Its documented malformed native responses
motivate keeping actual format validity separate from count choice.

The missing test is therefore a causal **presentation × delivery** crossing
with the same reporting relationship. The chosen minimal design keeps the
system/user/assistant-read_log/tool-response roles and tool schemas fixed in all
four cells. The log result's content is prose versus a JSON entry; the requested
current delivery is a plain integer versus a submit_report call. The source
remains a tool-result channel in the prose cell. This does not test removal of
all tool history, a quoted-user-log channel, or a role/authority change.

An engineering subset may repeat old contexts only to check exact weights and
numerical parity. It is explicitly not fresh confirmation. Fresh materials
exclude used/reserved banks, including the completed instruction-gating bank.
No previous outputs or reports are modified.
