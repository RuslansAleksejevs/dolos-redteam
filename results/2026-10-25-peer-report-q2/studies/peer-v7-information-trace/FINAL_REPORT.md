## What this does and does not establish

All-source removal changes the original prior-versus-truth margin by +0.075915 [+0.056562,+0.096752]. Nevertheless donor-specific P transfer is +0.000073 [-0.002100,+0.002146], and prior-report-minus-timer transfer is -0.000927 [-0.003444,+0.001452]. The frozen useful-transfer gates were raw>=0.10nat and report-minus-timer>=0.05nat, together with positive removal and bidirectional/quadruple consistency. No threshold was retuned after results.

Removing a contribution can change the answer even if swapping it between different reported numbers does not transfer those numbers. The result is consistent with an early contribution affecting subsequent processing or readout rather than directly transporting a specific prior number. It does not prove a generic response-preparation function, prove zero encoded information, or locate the report representation elsewhere. Small donor differences and nonlinear interactions can remain. No new layer/head/component search follows.

The isolated intervention preserves recipient ordinary values, Q/K and ordinary attention computation. Full donor attention is used only inside the inserted learned-delta path. Source states can contain contextual information, and a pre-report position can depend on later content only through later query/attention mixing, not through retrocausal value states. The primary result does not replace the entire residual stream.

## Stopping and limitations

No frozen source group, including all sources, passed donor-number information discovery. Independent confirmation, count-matched random-source controls, norm-matched random-direction confirmation, value-versus-mixing factorization, full-suffix confirmation and new unrestricted generation are **NOT RUN**, not negative test results. Their reserved materials remain untouched. We stop rather than lower the threshold or subdivide more sources.

The old trace reserve remains unscored. Nine new discovery quadruples and a fixed JSON/tool scaffold bound the interpretation. A failure of this direct local-transfer experiment does not establish that all information must be absent from the delta or that no downstream interaction matters. It does block a positive claim that the tested learned V7 path specifically conveys the donor prior number.
