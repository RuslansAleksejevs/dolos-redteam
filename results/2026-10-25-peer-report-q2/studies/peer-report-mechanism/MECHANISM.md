# What this run establishes

**Behavioral finding.** Under an imposed tool-call answer prefix, Dolos assigns a
larger conditional share to a false peer-reported count than Qwen. A matching false
number in an unrelated timer action has very little influence. The result survives
independent substitutions of the true and peer counts. It is not yet native generation.

**Information localization.** Broad final-prefix residual patches produce modest
donor-number shifts, especially around blocks 20–22. Literal digit readouts at user,
peer, action and prefix spans are retained as descriptive evidence only. Neither
result identifies a dedicated retrieval mechanism. The prefix-colon intervention
does not directly manipulate the subsequent numeral-prediction positions.

**Causal necessity.** Not established. No peer-span-specific head contribution was
selectively removed while preserving neutral capability. Whole-state changes are
not evidence of necessity of any particular information channel.

**Causal sufficiency.** Not established for a localized peer-specific component.
Whole residual-state transfer is a broad intervention. Some unrelated-action donor
patches increase false preference, showing that their effects cannot simply be read
as importing the donor's reporting policy.

**Training comparison.** The pinned models differ in the magnitude of the conditional
preference, and both exhibit it. No validated component-level difference, benign-QA
circuit comparison or surgical restoration is claimed. Earlier block-27 punctuation
results were not reopened and cannot explain this result by assumption.

**Intent and objective.** No evidence here establishes deception, collusive intent,
coordination, a hidden objective, or a reference-free audit. Relevant tool-action
imitation, prompt-format effects and ordinary fine-tuning changes remain alternatives.

**Stopping decision.** Only broad final-prefix residual transfer was obtained.
According to the requested gate, stop before exhaustive head searching, selective
suppression or confirmatory held-out validation. Those stages are explicitly NOT_RUN,
not failed experiments and not negative evidence about the existence of a hidden objective.
